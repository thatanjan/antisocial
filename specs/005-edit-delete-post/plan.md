## Summary

This feature allows post owners to edit the text of their posts and delete posts entirely. Options will be accessible via a dropdown menu (three dots) on each post. Editing is limited to text only, and deleting a post will also remove its associated image storage records. Confirmation modals will be used for destructive actions.
